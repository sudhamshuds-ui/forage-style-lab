# Apple

Sources: painterly session (2026-10-17), commits ba5157f..5b06b0c, memory note on painterly taste,
the ingredient brief of 2026-11-04 (facets retired).

## Colour
- **The ingredient keeps its own colours; only the room changes** (apple).
  First try: Randomize also shuffled the apple's colours.
  User said: "lets not do randomize colours...its too much and i want the ingredients to look like their colours".
  Ended on: room combos only (wall, table, light, shadow); Randomize never touches colour.
  Why: an apple must read as an apple at a glance; the room is where colour can play.
- **The room complements the fruit, softly lit, never candy** (apple).
  First try: muted rooms unrelated to the fruit, then the references' saturated pinks and blues.
  User said: "the backdrop colours dont compliment" the apple; the light should go from "blasted by sun" to "softly lit".
  Ended on: soft and vivid room families chosen to sit opposite the fruit on the colour wheel, muted and light.
  Why: a warm saturated subject in a cool, quiet room is what the source paintings do.
- **Shadows are a mix of colours, never one grey** (apple).
  First try: a single-colour cast shadow.
  User said: "how the ref has green blues mixed in the shadwos...purple pinks on the wedges".
  Ended on: violet and pink in the fruit's shadows, teal and lavender in the cast shadow, a faint prism at the light's edge; subtle by default.
  Why: coloured shadows are what make it a painting instead of a render.

## Composition
- **The room is one seamless surface that turns with the ingredient** (apple).
  First try: a flat backdrop that stayed still while the apple and shadow rotated, with a hard horizon.
  User said: "dont have a distinct horizon...just make a seamless infinite base...with mixed colours".
  Ended on: a 3D studio sweep (floor curving into wall), the light fixed in the room.
  Why: when only the subject turns, the picture reads as a sticker on a poster.

## Form
- **Smooth, rounded forms; no facets** (apple).
  First try: a faceted apple, liked at first ("i liked when the apple showed its poly edges").
  Ended on: facets retired for every ingredient (the brief of 2026-11-04); the switch is hidden and off.
  Why: one consistent soft look across all ingredients; shading does the work, not polygons.

## Recurring errors
- **Highlights are believable touches of light, never graphic shapes** (apple).
  First try: a two-pane window highlight.
  User said: "the window highlight doesnt look natural" and "they dont need to be such prominent shapes".
  Ended on: Soft sheen and Dry speckle; window shapes kept but never the default.
  Why: hard white shapes read as stickers on a soft painting.
- **No stamped marks on the fruit** (apple).
  First try: capsule dabs painted on the apple.
  User said: "the dabs look wierd".
  Ended on: texture that lives in the skin and paint grain, not stamped shapes.
  Why: stamps repeat and float; paint texture belongs to the surface.
- **Shadows stay clear shapes in soft light** (apple).
  User said: "i dont want it to look like shadows are non existant or blurred out."
  Ended on: soft light, but a clear cast shadow, darkest at the contact.
  Why: soft light is about the lit side, not about losing the shadow.
- **Patches and halos where layers meet** (apple).
  User said: "there are patches like this" (blocky variation on the soft apple).
  Ended on: smooth variation, no gap between the apple and its shadow, no light halo at the edge.
  Why: any seam between two layers reads as a cut-out.
