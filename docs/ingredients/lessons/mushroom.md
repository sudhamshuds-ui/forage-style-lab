# Mushrooms

Sources: commits on `r3-batch-1` (2026-11-15), the user's references (Figma node 184:349),
`docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md` and `batch-0-1-round-2-feedback.md`. Seven chestnut mushrooms
in a small heap. Approved on the third look.

## Colour
- **Where parts meet, the colours blend** (mushroom).
  First try: flat brown caps and pale stems. User said: "their texture needs a lot of improvement".
  Ended on: chestnut washes on the cap, pink-brown gill lines, a cream stem with tan fibres, each fading into the next (`mushroom` skin), plum-brown shadows.

## Composition
- **A group is a composition, not a scatter** (mushroom).
  First try: mushrooms standing upright, apart. User said: "the way they are placed feels wrong, some are levitating and upright. the composition isnt working".
  Ended on: a heap, every mushroom tipped over on its cap's rim and leaning in, touching its neighbours, stems and gills showing.
- **More pieces, each turned at random** (mushroom).
  User said: "mushrooms are fine, maybe increase number, and random rotation".
  Ended on: seven instead of five, each with its own turn.

## Form
- **A tipped piece rests on two points: solve the tilt** (mushroom, chicken).
  Claude's note: each mushroom's tilt is found by bisection so the cap's rim and the stem's end both touch the table; a guessed angle leaves one end in the air.
- **One outline spun round its axis** (mushroom).
  Claude's note: a domed cap, a rim rolling under, gills rising in to a stout stem (`spinProfile`).

## Recurring errors
- **An outline spun from the top down comes out inside out** (mushroom, milk, avocado).
  Claude's note: the signed volume was negative; the triangle order was flipped.
- **Levitating** (mushroom, onion, spinach).
  Claude's note: look at every piece's contact from low down.
