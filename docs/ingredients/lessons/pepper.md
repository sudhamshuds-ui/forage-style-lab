# Orange pepper

Sources: commits on branch `pepper` (2026-11-12), the user's reference painting (a yellow bell
pepper in oil) and notes in a cloud session.

## Colour
- **Close relatives must read apart, again** (pepper, orange).
  Claude's note: a new `pepper` family, a purer, glossier, slightly redder orange than the orange
  fruit's, so the two never look like the same ingredient.
- **Shadows on orange stay brick red, never pink** (pepper).
  First try: the shadow side leaned pink (the shared violet and pink shadow patches, and a
  magenta-red shadow colour).
  Ended on: a brick-red shadow colour and a third of the patches (`shadowTint` 0.3).
  Why: pink in an orange's shadow reads as a stain, not as light.
- **Glossy skins get long reflections that follow the form, never round blobs** (pepper, strawberry, onion).
  First try: the shared sheen put round pale patches on every lobe.
  Ended on: the shared sheen quiet (`matte`), and the gloss painted by the skin: soft streaks down
  the lit lobes (narrow across, where the lobe curves fast; long down it), broken by the skin's grain.
  Why: a pepper's reflection is a long stripe down each lobe; round blobs read as plastic.
- **Grooves are a soft gradient** (pepper, onion).
  Claude's note: deeper and a touch redder in the grooves, fading out over the lobe, never a line.

## Composition
- **A single, upright, three-quarter view** (pepper).
  Claude's note: one pepper standing on its feet, turned so a groove and a full lobe both face the
  light. Nothing new for several pieces.

## Form
- **On a blocky form, keep organic variation to about 5%** (pepper).
  First try: four even lobes (approved as a shape). User said: "the overall form is right, can be a bit more organically deformed... right now the body is very cylindrical...add a bit more deform".
  Second try: lobes of clearly different sizes and bulges, a strong lean, a taper. User said: "its too deformed..make the body like the prev version with 5% deform".
  Ended on: round 1's blocky body with each lobe a touch its own size and bulge, each groove a touch its own depth, and the lobes turning only slightly.
  Why: a little variation reads as grown; a lot reads as squashed. Start small (the butter's lesson, "start at about half", holds: here even less).
- **Per-part variation blends, never jumps** (pepper).
  Claude's note: sizes picked lobe by lobe jumped where two lobes met and left a stepped, jagged groove. Each lobe's size and each groove's depth are now weighted by how close a point is to it, so they blend smoothly round the body.
- **A stem from the reference: thick, flared, hooked** (pepper).
  User said: "also the stem can be better, sednig a ref".
  Ended on: a thick stem flaring out of a star-shaped calyx, rising and arching over like a hook, its end cut with a small soft bevel. Kept when the body went back to round 1.
- **Feet and a well** (pepper).
  Claude's note: the lobes' bottoms push down into four soft feet it stands on (the grooves lift off the table); the shoulders dip into a deep well round the stem.

## Recurring errors
- **Stepped edges where parts meet** (pepper; the cheese's sawtooth bevels).
  Claude's note: anything chosen per part (a lobe, a section) must blend across the boundary.
- **Flat pale highlight patches** (pepper, onion, strawberry, apple).
  Claude's note: on a glossy or satin skin, check the sheen first; paint the gloss in the skin when its shape matters.
- **The dev server dies during long sessions** (process).
  Claude's note: run it as a managed background task, and make render scripts retry when the page reloads.
