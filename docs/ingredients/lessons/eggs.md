(Figma node 184:349, then photos of a real half-dozen pulp carton), `docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md`

## Colour
- **Many of a kind: each egg its own brown** (eggs).
  Claude's note: each egg has its own shape, tilt and tone, with fine speckles (`eggshell` skin). User said: "Eggs look fine".
- **A grey material is never a dead grey** (eggs).
  Claude's note: the pulp is a warm grey-cream with lilac-blue shadows and soft fibres (`pulp`, part-only).

## Form
- **A real manufactured object is built the way it is made, from a photo of the real one** (eggs).
  First try: a simple box tray. User said: "the carton shape and form isnt right, its too simple".
  Then pointed peaks from a painting. User said: "Egg carton form is wrong see refs" (photos).
  Then an invented outer wall. User said: "egg carton corners are a bit wrong", "they are coming out", then "why are u curving the egg carton like that in the corner, also the height. please 1:1 the form of the carton with ref."
  Ended on: a thin flat rim plate, six separate round cups under it, two flat-topped posts down the middle, the lid standing open on its hinge.
  Why: each guess added a part the real thing does not have. Copy the real parts one for one and invent nothing.
- **Contents sit inside their holder** (eggs).
  Claude's note: check that no egg pokes through a wall from any side.
