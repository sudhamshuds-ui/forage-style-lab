# Potato

Sources: commits on `r3-batch-1` (2026-11-15), the user's references (Figma node 184:349),
`docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md` and `batch-0-1-round-2-feedback.md`. Two potatoes. Approved on
the second look: "Tomato, potato, peach perfect. done".

## Colour
- **Rough painted texture carries form on a matte surface** (potato, watermelon).
  First try: even golden washes. User said: "maybe give them more texture (similar to what we did for watermelon, those rough textures), they look very flat in shading rn".
  Ended on: a rough dry-brush texture over golden to russet washes, pale scuffs, earthy specks and a few eyes (`potato` skin).
  Why: with no shine to describe it, a matte form needs texture to show where it turns.

## Composition
- **A pair: one in front, one behind and to the side, just touching** (potato, peach).
  Claude's note: the `cluster-2` layout, each potato its own seeded shape and turn.

## Form
- **A lumpy thing is lopsided on purpose** (potato).
  Claude's note: an oblong on its side, one end fuller, broad soft lumps and a shallow waist. User said: "Potatoes look fine".
