# Lime

Sources: commits on `r3-batch-1` (2026-11-15; the kept version is commit 5d1c87b), the user's
references (Figma node 184:349 and a painted wedge), `docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md` and
`batch-0-1-round-2-feedback.md`. A whole lime and two wedges. Locked.

## Colour
- **Cut flesh needs light inside it** (lime).
  First try: a noisy pattern on the wedge faces. User said: "the cut pieces are not. the texture is glitching or wrong", "add some translucency and subsurface to the flesh".
  Ended on: a pale, soft fan of pulp with light inside it and a soft pith rim.
  Why: a noise pattern on a cut face can read as a rendering glitch.
- **After two failed tries, go back to the version that worked** (lime, a decision).
  Two tries at more detail (sacs, segment walls, a thick pith) failed. User said: "lime cuts still missing this details", "Lime this isnt the texture", then "its fine i dont think u can do it for lime, just go back to this version".
  Ended on: the wedge flesh of commit 5d1c87b, restored exactly. Do not add wedge detail again unless asked.

## Composition
- **Whole and cut: the whole one behind, the wedges in front on a cut face** (lime).
  Claude's note: each wedge rests flat on a cut side, its flesh toward the camera.
