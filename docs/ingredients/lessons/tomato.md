# Tomato

Sources: commits on `r3-batch-1` (2026-11-15), the user's references (Figma node 184:349),
`docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md` and `batch-0-1-round-2-feedback.md`. Approved on the second
look: "Tomato, potato, peach perfect. done".

## Colour
- **A red that reads apart from the other reds** (tomato).
  Claude's note: scarlet, more orange than the apple and hotter than the melon; orange shoulders, deeper crimson blooms, wine-violet shadows (`tomato` family and skin).

## Composition
- **One solo tomato, its calyx turned toward the camera** (tomato).
  Claude's note: the calyx is the signature, so it shows from the starting view.

## Form
- **The reference's form comes first, even on an easy shape** (tomato, peach, garlic, eggs).
  First try: a plain round body with a flat little star. User said: "improve form a bit according to ref. The leaves can get more detail and form similar to ref".
  Ended on: soft ribs running down from the stem, five long sepals curling up and back on a star base, a knuckled stem.
  Why: a lathe with good colour is not enough; the signature has to be built and pushed.
- **Sculpt from the foot, not from the middle** (tomato, peach, garlic).
  Claude's note: a body from `roundBody` is centred on its middle. The ribs measured height from zero and did nothing until they used `y + half`.

## Recurring errors
- **A sculpting pass that silently does nothing** (tomato, peach, garlic).
  Claude's note: when a change does not show in the render, check the coordinates it measures from before making it stronger.
