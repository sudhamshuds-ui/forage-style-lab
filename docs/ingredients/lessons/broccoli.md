# Broccoli

Sources: session "3D ingredient forms" (2026-11-04), commits 9578bec..4392af2.

## Colour
- **Matte where the real thing is matte** (broccoli).
  Claude's note: the crown caught a loud highlight; `matte: true` keeps the body's highlight quiet (also bread, cheese, butter, melon).
  Why: a shiny crown reads as plastic.

## Composition
- **Lying down, resting the way it really would** (broccoli).
  First try: a head standing upright like a tree.
  User said: "not standing uopright" and "make the lower stem touch teh ground...like lay it opposite".
  Ended on: one head lying on its side, crown and cut end both resting on the table, a leaf on the table.
  Why: a broccoli on a counter lies down; standing up it reads as a tree.
- **Pieces never pass through each other** (broccoli).
  User said: "the composition they are like going thru eachother".
  Ended on: stalks are solid in the layout check; they fan apart and never cross.
  Why: interpenetration breaks belief instantly.

## Form
- **A crown is many bumps, not a cap** (broccoli).
  First try: a smooth blob crown.
  User said: "this looks klike a tree/bush", "it needs more bumps....like currently it looks like a mushroom", "top still needs like 70% more bump".
  Ended on: about 115 lumps blended into one surface (surface nets), a fuller dome with an inner ring.
  Why: the bumpy crown is the broccoli's whole identity; when in doubt, push the signature feature further.
- **Branches are short, slim fingers** (broccoli).
  User said: "make these small, theya re too big right now".
  Ended on: a higher fork, shorter and slimmer finger branches, a ribbed stalk with leaf scars.
  Why: thick branches make it read as a tree trunk.

## Recurring errors
- **Anything lying at table height flickers with the floor** (broccoli, butter).
  User said: "glitching here" (the leaf on the table).
  Ended on: the leaf rests just above the table; later, flat pieces became props drawn a hair in front of the floor.
  Why: two surfaces at the same depth fight, frame by frame.
- **Meshed bumps need a clean mesh, no ledges** (broccoli).
  Claude's note: the blob crown showed stepped ledges; surface nets turn any distance field into a clean mesh.
