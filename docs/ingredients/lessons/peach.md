# Peach

Sources: commits on `r3-batch-1` (2026-11-15), the user's references (Figma node 184:349),
`docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md` and `batch-0-1-round-2-feedback.md`. Two peaches. Approved on
the second look: "Tomato, potato, peach perfect. done".

## Colour
- **Velvet, never glossy** (peach).
  Claude's note: gold to rose blush, a pale bloom at the outline, rose-violet shadows, a quiet highlight (`peach` skin, `matte`). User said: "The textures and colour are perfect."

## Composition
- **A pair, the cleft of the front one toward the camera** (peach).
  Claude's note: the `cluster-2` layout, turned so the seam shows from the starting view.

## Form
- **Build the signature feature and push it until it reads** (peach, tomato, broccoli).
  First try: a round ball with a dip at the stem. User said: "they are missing their iconic peach bump. refer refs for the form."
  Ended on: a soft cleft down one side from stem to tip, a fuller cheek each side of it.
  Why: without the cleft and cheeks it is an apricot or a ball.
- **Sculpt from the foot, not from the middle** (peach, tomato, garlic).
  Claude's note: the cleft did nothing at first because height was measured from zero on a centred body.

## Recurring errors

  Claude's note: see the tomato's record.
