## Form
- **Many small things must read as many small things** (rice).
  First try: a smooth mound with grains painted on. User said: "i want some visible grain forms seen, match the ref, it looks like 1 blob of icecream rn".
  Ended on: real grain forms on the mound.
- **Detail yes, noise no: small pieces sit in the mass** (rice).
  Second try: many raised grains, each catching its own light. User said: "i like the grains, but its too much noise...either blend them a bit into the main form."
  Ended on: fewer grains (380), each sunk partly into the mound so they share its light.
  Why: hundreds of raised pieces read as static; half-sunk pieces read as a heap of grains.
