(Figma node 184:349), `docs/ingredients/batch-1-feedback.md` and `batch-0-1-round-2-feedback.md`. A

## Colour
- **White food: cream where lit, lilac-violet in shadow** (garlic, rice, milk).
  Claude's note: violet streaks from root to neck, cream blooms, a tan root, gold on the cloves (`garlic` skin).

## Composition
- **Loose pieces stay close to their parent** (garlic, blueberries).
  First try: the cloves sat well away from the bulb. User said: "the cloves are fine, but too far apart".
  Ended on: both cloves near the bulb's foot, almost touching it.

## Form
- **A neck is slim and grows out of the shoulders** (garlic, onion).
  First try: a thick, upright neck. User said: "the top part is like a tree trunk too thick and upright, improve the form there, see ref".
  Ended on: a slim papery neck, the shoulders pulling up into it.
- **Change only what the note asks about** (garlic, salmon).
  The fix also reshaped the bulb. User said: "garlic looks squashed now, go back to prev form. just make the top part thinner- the cylindrical form".
  Ended on: the earlier bulb exactly, with only the neck thinner.
